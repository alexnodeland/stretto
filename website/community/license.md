## The published data

The results in `docs/results/` publish rows, flows and recorded episodes beside the code. Two kinds of file there carry terms of their own:

- **The answer bundles** (`answers-*.jsonl.gz`, `phase0b-*-answers.jsonl.gz`) hold answers of TypeSafe's Jev. They are provided only to reproduce and audit stretto's analysis; each bundle's page states TypeSafe's terms ([an example](../../docs/results/answers-2026-09-25-telecom.md#terms)).

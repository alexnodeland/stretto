# Code of conduct

stretto follows the [Contributor Covenant, version 2.1](https://www.contributor-covenant.org/version/2/1/code_of_conduct/).

In short: be respectful and considerate, welcome people of every background and level of experience, disagree about ideas rather than people, and assume good faith. Harassment, insults, and unwelcome attention of any kind are not accepted, in any project space, on or off GitHub.

To report a problem, contact the maintainer privately through GitHub (a private security advisory works for anything sensitive). Reports are handled confidentially. The maintainer may remove comments, commits or other contributions that break this code, and may ban people who do so repeatedly or seriously, as the Covenant's enforcement guidelines describe.

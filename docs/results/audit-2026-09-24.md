# Flow audit, 2026-09-24: the goal-free flows on GLM-5's test episodes

`stretto audit` scores recorded episodes under a flow. At every decision the flow would face (right after a lookup returns), the flow's arbiter gives a distribution over the site's options: the lookups offered there, handing back, and the rest for a step it does not offer. Those choices form one fugue program, a categorical `sample` per decision. Run with fugue's `ScoreGivenTrace` on a trace of the agent's own steps, it scores what the agent did: the log-probability of its path, which is how surprised the flow is. The same program run with `PriorHandler` simulates the flow as a stochastic policy.

Here the flows the live pilots used (compiled goal-free from the 2025 baselines) are audited against GLM-5's published τ²-bench trajectories, on the test split only (tasks the flows never trained on), with the replay cache. The replay check had already asked every question, so this cost nothing:

```bash
stretto audit --flow flows/retail.json \
  --results .data/tau2-targets/glm-5_enabled_retail_gpt-5.2_4trials.json \
  --tau2 ../tau2-bench --oracle replay --oracle-cache .oracle-cache
```

## What it shows

- **Retail fits; airline does not.** The flow's likeliest option was GLM-5's own step at 85.9% of 724 retail decisions (0.42 nats per decision), and at 64.5% of 228 airline decisions (1.02 nats). The retail figure matches the offline agreement for GLM-5 (85.7% with predicates, RFC-001 §3.13). The airline one is the audit's warning: GLM-5 in τ²-bench's harness reads several reservations in one turn (it makes parallel calls in 45% of its airline tool turns), while the flow decides one lookup at a time. After a reservation read it agrees 42% of the time. This matches the offline projection, which gave flows under 5% of GLM-5's airline turns.
- **Calibration runs opposite ways.** In retail the flow is underconfident: when it gives its top option 0.6–0.7, that option is right 80% of the time, and 0.8–0.9 is right 96% of the time. In airline it is overconfident: 0.8–0.9 is right 74% of the time. A deployment could recalibrate on its own sessions. The audit says which way.
- **Where to look.** The hardest retail site is after an order is read (61% agreement): read another order, or answer? The episodes the flow fits least are listed, so a person can read them.

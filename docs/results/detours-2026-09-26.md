# What is left of the detours, and three fixes that do not work

After [the named-other score](named-other-2026-09-26.md), GLM-5's airline test episodes see 6 detours for 55 turns saved, but an agent D0 never trained on sees many more: Claude Sonnet 4.5's airline test episodes see 63 detours for 130 turns saved, and its retail ones 82 for 412. This page asks where those come from, and tries three remedies from the literature on the replays: hand the session back once the flow is surprised ([#17](https://github.com/alexnodeland/stretto/issues/17)), stop a chain of lookups when their joint probability falls (speculative decoding's rule), and learn the flow from the served agent's own traces. None cuts them. What is left is a semantic stop: once the reservation the customer described has been read, Sonnet reads no more and GLM-5 reads on.

All numbers are D0, recompiled with the named-other score, served habit-only at 0.3, replayed on the test tasks' four trials (80 airline and 160 retail episodes per agent), unless a row says otherwise.

## Handing the session back does not help

Surprise is measured after the fact, and here after the fact is too late. The rule was simulated on the replays: once it fires, the flow makes no more lookups in that session, and the agent makes the calls the flow would have made. Even an oracle, told of each detour the moment the flow made it, leaves most of them, because they come in one burst per session: several reads right after the user lookup, before anything surprising has happened.

| Rule | Airline: turns saved · detours · sessions handed back | Retail |
|---|---|---|
| None | 130 · 63 · 0 | 412 · 82 · 0 |
| Oracle, after the first detour | 130 · 57 · 32 | 412 · 82 · 53 |
| Oracle, after the second | 130 · 61 · 17 | 412 · 82 · 21 |
| After the agent's first call of a tool the flow had already looked up with other arguments | 130 · 62 · 10 | 412 · 80 · 9 |
| After the second | 130 · 63 · 6 | 412 · 82 · 4 |

So a per-session hand-back stays unbuilt, and #17 remains open for sessions that go off script later, which these are not.

## A chain's joint probability says nothing about use

Detours sit deep in chains: of Sonnet's airline detours, 16 were the first lookup after one of the agent's calls, and 47 the second or later. A draft tree in speculative decoding ranks each drafted token by the product of the drafter's confidences along its path ([EAGLE-2](https://arxiv.org/abs/2406.16858)), and [SpecDec++](https://arxiv.org/abs/2405.19715) stops drafting once the chance that some token is rejected passes a threshold. The flow's rule is local: each lookup's probability times its binding's chance must reach 0.3 on its own. With `check_flow.py --explore 0`, each lookup's outcome is known:

| Replay | Place in chain | Lookups | Probability × chance | Product along the chain | Used |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| GLM-5, airline | 1 | 71 | 0.43 | 0.43 | 92% |
| GLM-5, airline | 2 | 51 | 0.59 | 0.26 | 100% |
| GLM-5, airline | 4 or later | 45 | 0.68 | 0.09 | 100% |
| Sonnet 4.5, airline | 1 | 79 | 0.43 | 0.43 | 80% |
| Sonnet 4.5, airline | 2 | 46 | 0.59 | 0.27 | 52% |
| Sonnet 4.5, airline | 4 or later | 40 | 0.68 | 0.10 | 72% |

Two things follow. The flow's numbers are next-step probabilities, and a lookup is used if the agent needs it at any later step, so for GLM-5 they understate use by 0.4 to 0.5; for Sonnet they are about right at places 2 and 3 and low elsewhere. And the product along the chain predicts nothing: at 0.3 it would drop 135 of GLM-5's 206 airline lookups, every one of which was used, and 302 of its 612 retail ones (292 used). On Sonnet it drops 121 airline lookups, 74 of them used. A drafted token is wasted when any token before it is rejected, because a sequence must match as a whole; a read is not, because the agent can use it whenever it gets there. Lookups are a set, not a sequence, and chaining is not where the risk compounds.

## Learning from the agent's own traces, or asking Jev, does not either

| Flow | Airline: turns saved · detours | Retail |
|---|---|---|
| D0, the habit alone (four 2025 agents' traces) | 130 · 63 | 412 · 82 |
| Learned from Sonnet 4.5's own training episodes, habit alone | 128 · 58 | 408 · 89 |
| D0 with its arbiter (the habit and Jev's answers) | 117 · 59 | – |

A flow learned from 120 of Sonnet's own airline sessions (296 retail) does what D0 does. The arbiter asked Jev 615 questions the cache did not hold (about $0.05) and cut 4 detours for 13 fewer turns saved.

## What is left: stopping once the record is found

Of Sonnet's 51 airline detours on reservations, 20 came after the flow had already read a reservation whose origin and destination the customer had named (by city or airport code) or whose id they had given, and only 7 reads in that position were used. GLM-5 in the same position used 32 of 32: it reads every reservation whatever it has found. So the same decision point, with the same state, is right for one agent and wrong for the other, and the difference is whether the agent stops a search at the record the customer described. Matching "from New York to Seattle" to a record holding `JFK` and `SEA` is language, not string matching: flagging the same position by any of the record's field values the customer wrote, a rule a flow could apply to any server, separates nothing (10 detours against 41 uses).

That points to the arbiter's questions rather than to the habit: a predicate such as "has the record the customer described been read?" at the sites that iterate, learned per agent as the named-other score is. It is not built; `stretto refine --candidates` can measure one.

## Reproduce

The replays are `pilot/check_flow_tagged.py` (for `tags.json`), with `--explore 0` for the decisions; flows as in [the named-other page](named-other-2026-09-26.md); the own-trace flow is `stretto learn --results claude-sonnet-4-5_enabled_airline_gpt-5.2_4trials.json --habit-only`. Then [`scripts/detours.py`](../../scripts/detours.py):

```sh
python3 scripts/detours.py handback claude-sonnet-4-5_enabled_airline_gpt-5.2_4trials.json runs/tag-sonnet-airline
python3 scripts/detours.py chains runs/calib-glm-5-airline runs/calib-sonnet-airline
python3 scripts/detours.py described claude-sonnet-4-5_enabled_airline_gpt-5.2_4trials.json runs/calib-sonnet-airline --tau2 ../tau2-bench
```
